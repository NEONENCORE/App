Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

NEONENCORE (“we”, “the app”) is an offline entertainment app for practice-style mini-games themed around a live show.

Information on your device

Gameplay progress and preferences remain on your iPhone or iPad. The app is built to work without sending your scores or settings elsewhere.

Data stored on your device

Progress and preferences are saved locally using Apple’s UserDefaults, including for example:

  • Best scores and star ratings per game mode
  • Show Night combined scores and run counts
  • Local achievement flags
  • Last played mode and optional weekly setlist spotlight index
  • Backstage / opening screen completion flags

Weekly challenge content is derived from the ISO calendar week on your device (offline seeding).

What we do not do

  • No sale of personal data
  • No third-party advertising or ad tracking inside the app
  • No gambling, betting, or casino mechanics
  • No off-device copies of your gameplay data

Children

The app is designed as general entertainment and does not ask for your identity.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling NEONENCORE from your device removes locally stored progress and settings associated with the app.

Changes

We may update this policy if app behavior changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions about NEONENCORE, email NEONENCORE@outlook.com.
