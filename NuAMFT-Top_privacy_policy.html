Effective date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NuAMFT-Top (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our mobile game on iOS. We designed the App to work fully offline and to keep your experience on your device.

Summary NuAMFT-Top does not gather personal information, does not transmit your data to our servers, and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device only.

1. Information We Do Not Gather

We do not request, receive, or store:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
  • Location data
  • Photos, camera microphone, or contacts
  • Device identifiers for tracking or profiling

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To support gameplay, the App may save non-personal data locally using iOS on-device storage (for example, route progress, difficulty preference, sound and haptic settings, and high-score records). This information:

  • Remains on your iPhone or iPad
  • Is not uploaded to us or shared with third parties by the App
  • May be removed when you delete the App or reset app data through iOS settings

3. Internet and Third Parties

NuAMFT-Top is playable without an internet connection. The App does not include embedded analytics, advertising networks, or social sharing SDKs that receive your data.

If you open an external link from the App (such as a privacy policy page in Safari), that website is operated separately and governed by its own policies. We do not control third-party websites.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly gather personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal information is gathered, parental consent mechanisms for data submission are not applicable.

5. Data Security

Because data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, risk exposure through our systems is limited. You are responsible for securing your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID as you prefer.

6. Your Choices

  • Delete local data: Uninstall NuAMFT-Top from your device to remove locally stored game data.
  • Preferences: Sound and haptic options can be changed in the in-app Settings screen.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
